Response to Guideline 2.1 — build 23

1. Physical-device testing and video
Build 23 was tested by the developer on a physical iPhone 16 Pro (iOS 26.7.3): sign-in, navigation, document draft creation and document/PDF viewing; no subscription or purchase prompt appeared. Attached TEVEL-APP-REVIEW-EVIDENCE.zip contains tevel-main-flow.mp4 (83 seconds: Home Screen launch, sign-in, navigation, invoice creation/PDF, customers/reports/profile) and tevel-account-deletion.mp4 (29 seconds: launch, sign-in, account deletion and return to sign-in). Videos predate build 23, which additionally removes purchase prompts.

2. Purpose, audience and business model
Tevel centralizes customers, business documents, expenses and reports for Israeli business owners and their authorized staff. It reduces duplicate entry and keeps business records together. Businesses/accounts are provisioned by Tevel; the app has no self-service account registration. New customers contact Tevel from the sign-in screen. The app and ALL features are free for all users in this release. No subscription, in-app purchase or external payment is required for access.

3. Review access and instructions
Use the updated demo credentials in Sign-In Information. The dedicated business/customer records are fictitious. Internet access is required; no accessory or sample file is needed for the main flow. Home (בית): tap +, choose document type, enter amount, select/add a customer, review the summary and issue; open its document/PDF. Customers (לקוחות): manage customers. Management (ניהול): business tools, expenses and reports. Profile (פרופיל): account/business settings. Features follow business-role permissions.
Account deletion: Profile > account settings > Delete account (מחיקת חשבון); enter the current account password and type מחק, then confirm. No Apple authentication is required. Access is removed and the user is signed out; business records subject to legal retention can remain. Test deletion last because the credentials then stop working.
The app has private business records and user-directed document sharing, with no public community feed or random-user chat.

4. External services
Tevel production API: Oracle-hosted PostgreSQL and file storage; Supabase Auth: primary email/password authentication; Gmail SMTP: requested business email; Apple APNs: enabled push notifications; ClamAV: uploaded-file scanning. User-directed sharing can open Mail/WhatsApp or the iOS share sheet. Camera/photos/contacts/calendar permissions relate to the selected feature. No AI provider is active. Apple/Cardcom purchases are disabled.

5. Regions
The feature set is consistent across storefronts. The interface is Hebrew and the workflows target Israeli businesses, with ILS amounts. Documents are not adapted to other countries' tax systems.

6. Authorization and third-party material
This release offers business record keeping and invoicing, not banking, lending, investment or payment-processing services. The developer reports registration with SHAAM; no software-registration certificate is supplied with this submission and no government approval is claimed. Automatic Israel Tax Authority submission is disabled. The app does not distribute licensed third-party media.
